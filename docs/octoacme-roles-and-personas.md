# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads own quality strategy, test planning, and acceptance criteria validation. They ensure that features meet defined acceptance criteria and quality standards before release.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy and approach for each project
- Create and maintain test plans and acceptance criteria
- Execute and coordinate manual and automated testing
- Report quality metrics and blockers
- Collaborate with developers on testability and design
- Identify gaps in acceptance criteria

### Goals
- Ensure all features meet acceptance criteria before release
- Minimize bugs reaching production
- Provide clear quality signals to the team and stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and refinement sessions
- Daily standups (quality updates)
- Code review comments (testability feedback)
- Weekly quality reports

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Developers**: Collaborate on test cases, testability design, and reproduction of issues
- **Product Managers**: Clarify acceptance criteria and validate feature completeness
- **Project Managers**: Report blockers and quality risks; coordinate test timelines

---

## Stakeholder/Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and sponsors are business owners and decision makers who provide strategic direction, budget approval, and organizational support. They ensure the project delivers business value and aligns with strategic goals.

### Responsibilities
- Provide business context and success metrics
- Approve project charter, budget, and timeline
- Make key business decisions and trade-offs
- Remove organizational blockers
- Monitor project health and ROI

### Goals
- Ensure project delivers measurable business value
- Align project with organizational strategy
- Minimize scope creep and budget overruns

### Typical Communication
- Milestone reviews and approvals
- Monthly or quarterly stakeholder updates
- Risk escalations and decisions
- Post-project retrospectives and impact reviews

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Project Managers**: Receive status updates; provide approvals and escalation resolution
- **Product Managers**: Align on business objectives and success metrics
- **Developers**: Engage for technical feasibility assessments and major decisions

---

## Technical Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Architects design technical solutions, evaluate technical trade-offs, and ensure systems are scalable, maintainable, and aligned with long-term strategy.

### Responsibilities
- Design technical solutions for complex features
- Identify and mitigate technical risks
- Review designs and architectures for scalability and maintainability
- Provide guidance on tech stack and tooling decisions
- Plan for technical debt and refactoring

### Goals
- Ensure systems are scalable, maintainable, and performant
- Reduce technical risk and technical debt
- Support long-term strategic technical direction

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews
- Architecture decision records (ADRs)
- Sprint planning (technical feasibility)
- Risk and mitigation planning

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Developers**: Collaborate on design; provide architectural guidance and reviews
- **Project Managers**: Identify technical risks and timelines for complex work
- **Product Managers**: Advise on feasibility and trade-offs

---

## Scrum Master/Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters and Agile Coaches facilitate agile ceremonies, remove impediments, and coach teams on agile practices. They enable teams to work effectively and continuously improve.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint ceremonies (planning, standup, review, retrospective)
- Identify and help remove blockers and impediments
- Coach team on agile practices and continuous improvement
- Track team metrics and health indicators
- Support organizational change and adoption of agile practices

### Goals
- Enable team productivity and self-organization
- Foster a culture of continuous improvement
- Remove obstacles to delivery
- Build strong, high-performing teams

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and retrospectives
- One-on-one coaching sessions
- Agile metrics and health reports
- Coaching discussions with leadership

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Developers**: Support in removing blockers and improving team processes
- **Project Managers**: Collaborate on sprint planning and risk management
- **Product Managers**: Ensure backlog refinement and prioritization alignment
- **All Roles**: Facilitate communication and psychological safety

---

## UX/Design Lead

### Role Summary
UX/Design Leads define user experience, conduct design reviews, and ensure usability standards are met. They advocate for users and help translate user needs into effective designs.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and define user personas and journeys
- Create wireframes, prototypes, and design specifications
- Conduct design reviews and usability testing
- Ensure consistency with design system and standards
- Collaborate with developers on implementation fidelity
- Document design decisions and rationale

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, usable products that delight users
- Reduce support burden through better design
- Maintain design consistency across products
- Drive user satisfaction and adoption

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and critique sessions
- User research findings and insights
- Usability testing results and recommendations
- Weekly collaboration with developers and product managers

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Developers**: Collaborate on implementation, design system consistency, and accessibility
- **Product Managers**: Align on user needs, priorities, and success metrics
- **Project Managers**: Coordinate design timelines and dependencies
- **QA/Testing Lead**: Collaborate on usability testing and acceptance criteria

---

## Security/Compliance Officer

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Officers ensure that projects meet security standards, regulatory requirements, and risk mitigation goals. They protect organizational assets and customer data.

### Responsibilities
- Define security requirements and standards
- Review designs and code for security vulnerabilities
- Ensure compliance with regulatory requirements
- Manage security incidents and escalations
- Provide security guidance and training
- Maintain security documentation and audit trails

### Goals
- Minimize security vulnerabilities and data breaches
- Ensure compliance with applicable regulations
- Build security-conscious culture
- Protect organizational reputation and customer trust

### Typical Communication
- Security design reviews
- Code review comments (security checks)
- Risk assessments and incident response
- Security training and awareness sessions
- Compliance reporting and audits

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Developers**: Collaborate on secure coding practices and vulnerability remediation
- **Project Managers**: Escalate security risks; coordinate incident response
- **Stakeholders**: Report security posture and compliance status
- **Technical Architects**: Advise on secure system design and architecture
